Our Story

Farm Scouts was born from a simple idea: to support farmers with smart tools that make their lives easier and more profitable. In a time when climate change, pests, and unpredictable markets affect agriculture deeply, we knew it was time to innovate.

Our team brings together agricultural experts, engineers, and local farmers to design IoT-powered tools that detect pests early, automate fertilizer use, and monitor field health—using real-time data from your land. Whether you're in a remote village or managing a large farm, Farm Scouts helps you make informed decisions from your phone.

Together, we can protect crops, reduce costs, and ensure a more sustainable farming future for India and beyond.
